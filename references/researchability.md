# Researchability Record

This is the single authority for the first-page Researchability Record.

| Field | Exact meaning and rule |
|---|---|
| Information richness | Evidence coverage: **A** has the latest filing, sufficient multi-year, segment, and market evidence, plus critical cross-checks; **B** has the latest filing but material inference or coverage gaps; **C** lacks critical Tier 1/current-period evidence or has structurally sparse disclosure. |
| AI research confidence | Source/evidence reliability: A may be High/Medium/Low; B is at most Medium; C is at most Low. |
| Investment certainty | Business durability and predictability; independent from evidence coverage. |
| First-page Confidence | Decision confidence, derived conservatively from the evidence and thesis; do not duplicate it as a generic report-confidence label elsewhere. |

When AI research confidence and investment certainty appear to diverge, add one sentence explaining why. Information scarcity is never, by itself, a negative investment verdict. The final section only confirms that AI research confidence and investment certainty are distinct.
